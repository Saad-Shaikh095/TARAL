### Key Scientific Results

The 75–150 m layer is the main weakness. Errors rise above 1°C and peak at 100 m, whereas surface predictions have RMSE around 0.53°C.

Deep-layer RMSE is much lower, but the model's improvement over climatology is small at 500–1000 m. For example, at 500 m, RMSE is 0.368°C versus a climatology RMSE of 0.377°C; the calculated RMSE skill is only 2.4%. High Pearson correlation at those depths therefore should not be mistaken for strong anomaly-prediction skill.
